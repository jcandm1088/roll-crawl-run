# Design System (Checkpoint 1, final)

The source of truth for colors is `src/ui/theme/themes.ts`. Spacing, type, and component styles are in `src/ui/styles/base.css`.

## Direction
Premium, warm, calm, and adult. Neutral backgrounds, one confident primary color per theme, soft rounded cards, generous spacing, serif display type paired with a clean sans. There are no cartoons or illustrations beyond the app mark. Progress and status will never use red or amber "warning" colors.

## Brand
- **Wordmark:** "Roll, Crawl, **RUN**" in Fraunces 500, with "RUN" in semibold in the theme's primary color. The subtitle "TRACKING GROWTH TOGETHER" sits below in Inter 600, uppercase, tracked at 0.16em. Capitalization is preserved exactly.
- **App mark: “Growing Together” (Concept 5, approved).** A parent and a child side by side, with the child in front. The single source is `src/ui/brand/growing-together.json`. The previous three-circle mark was retired at Checkpoint 1 acceptance; it is still in git history and in the concept board.
- **Installed icons always use the fixed brand colors** (background #EBDCCB, parent #7A4F33, child #C08A5E), whatever the active child's theme. The in-app mark uses the active theme (primary-soft, primary, accent).
- Icon files in `public/icons/`: icon-192, icon-512, icon-maskable-512 (artwork inset 14% for the safe zone), apple-touch-icon (180, full bleed), and favicon.svg. They are generated by `npm run icons` and referenced with `?v=2` so caches refresh. Small-size checks are in `docs/branding/icon-check-32.png` and `icon-check-16.png`.

## Typography (Option B — approved at Checkpoint 1 acceptance)
**Rule:** Inter is used for every functional heading, label, and body text. **Fraunces is used only for branding** (the wordmark: “Roll, Crawl, **RUN**” and its subtitle line). Never set a font on a single page; use the tokens and components below.

| Role | Token or class | Font / size / weight / line height / tracking |
|---|---|---|
| Primary page heading (every tab) | `PageHeader` → `.title-1` (`--h1-*`) | Inter 26px / 600 / 1.2 / −0.02em |
| Section heading, sheet title, accordion title | `.title-2`, `.accordion-title` (`--h2-*`) | Inter 20px / 600 / 1.3 / −0.015em |
| Top-bar screen title (Settings, profiles) | `.topbar-title` (`--h2-*`) | Inter 20px / 600 / 1.3 / −0.015em |
| Child's name on Home | `.child-name` | Inter 20px / 600 |
| Child's age on Home (precise: “2 years, 3 months, 14 days”; wraps only between parts) | `.child-age` | Inter 18px / 500 |
| Card title | `.title-3` | Inter 18px / 600 / 1.35 |
| Body | — | Inter 16px / 400 / 1.5 |
| Secondary | `.small` | Inter 14px / 400 / 1.45 |
| Caption, badge, eyebrow | `.xsmall`, `.badge`, `.eyebrow` | Inter 13px / 600 |
| Wordmark (branding) | `Wordmark` | Fraunces 500, with “RUN” in 600 |

History: Fraunces produced undesirable letterforms (J, f) in functional headings. Option A (selective Inter) was tried at the first review, and Option B was approved at the second review.

## Spacing, shape, elevation
- 4px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 56. The phone side gutter is 16px and card padding is 20px.
- Radii: 10 (small), 14 (inputs, choice cards), 20 (cards), 24 (sheet top corners), and pill for buttons, chips, and segmented controls.
- Shadows are soft and tinted by the theme: `card` (1px plus a 20px blur) and `float` (sheets, banners).
- Content is centered with a 560px maximum width on tablets and desktops.

## Components
- **Buttons:** pill-shaped, 48px tall. Variants: primary (filled), secondary (soft tint), outline, ghost (text), and danger. Danger uses a neutral outline rather than red, because destructive actions are protected by typed confirmation instead of alarming color. Pressing a button scales it slightly (0.98).
- **Inputs:** 48px tall, 1.5px strong outline, 14px radius, and a 3px focus halo. Errors show an icon and text, never color alone.
- **Choice cards** (radio-like) and **segmented controls** show selection with a soft tint and a check mark.
- **Cards:** surface background, hairline border, soft shadow. **Placeholder cards** use a dashed outline, no shadow, and a "Not available yet · Checkpoint N" tag, so they never look like working features.
- **Top bar:** sticky and translucent, with a backdrop blur. It holds the app mark (or a back button), the active-child chip (avatar, name, chevron), and the Settings button.
- **Bottom navigation:** five tabs with an icon and a text label, each at least 64px tall. The active tab uses the primary color, semibold text, and a small indicator bar, so it doesn't rely on color alone.
- **Sheets:** bottom sheets on phones and centered dialogs on wider screens. They close with Escape or a tap on the dimmed background.
- **Icons:** Lucide line icons at a 1.75px stroke, 22–24px.

## Components added at the Checkpoint 1 review
- **Child selector popover:** a dropdown that opens downward from just below the top bar, level with the top of the profile card. It is left-aligned to the chip, at most 360px wide, and its height is limited to the visible space below it, with the list scrolling inside. It is rendered through a portal into `<body>`, so no ancestor can affect its position. It traps focus, starting on the current child; arrow keys move between children, Enter selects, and Escape closes and returns focus to the chip.
- **Accordion:** a full-width header button containing the title (Fraunces), a one-line summary, and a chevron that rotates 180° when open. It uses `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and the panel is a labelled region. Accordions are collapsed by default and several can be open at once. The *subtle* variant (small Inter 600 muted title, hairline divider) is used for the “Advanced …” sections.
- **Quiet button (`btn-quiet`):** a transparent button with muted text and a hairline border. It is the entry point for destructive actions until the person deliberately continues.
- **Two-step destructive dialogs:** step 1 explains the consequences and requires a typed phrase; step 2 asks for a separate final confirmation. Cancel is always the filled primary button and is listed first.
- **Corrected-age tag:** a small muted pill reading “corrected” next to the corrected age on Home. Standard age has no label.
- **Sheets** now also render through a portal and trap focus.

## Motion & accessibility
- Screens fade and rise slightly on entry (240ms), sheets slide up, and theme colors cross-fade. All motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Layouts respect safe areas (`viewport-fit=cover` with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` padding).
- Touch targets are at least 48px. Radio groups have one Tab stop and support arrow, Home, and End keys. The focus ring is 3px in the theme's focus color.

## Checkpoint 2a components
- **Growth landing:** the shared `PageHeader`, a "For [child]" line, and four section cards in the approved order. Goals & Exercises stays a dashed placeholder (2b).
- **Sub-screens** (Progress, What to Expect, Milestone Library, About these milestones, Milestone): the top bar shows the title and a Back button. The Growth tab stays highlighted, and Back on a milestone returns to the previous screen.
- **Progress line:** a label, the raw fraction "N of M achieved", an 8px neutral meter in the theme's primary colour on the sunken track, and "Emerging · Not yet observed · hidden" counts. Neutral text ("Nothing to track yet", "All hidden") appears instead of a meter. No red, amber, grades, or percentiles.
- **Status badge:**
  - Not yet observed: outlined, muted.
  - Emerging: dashed, soft tint.
  - Achieved: filled primary, with a check icon.

  Status is shown by text, shape, and icon, not colour alone.
- **Milestone rows:** CDC text, an area label, and a status badge, grouped under "By X months" headings ("upcoming" when not yet applicable).
- **Info note:** a sunken panel with an info icon, used for safeguards (compact on Home, full on Progress).
- **CDC attribution card:** "Source: CDC", free availability, and non-endorsement. External links use an inline external-link icon with screen-reader text ("opens cdc.gov in a new window; needs internet").
- **Filter chips:** pill toggles with `aria-pressed`. Area and age filters use selects.
- **History timeline:** a vertical rule with dots. Each entry shows the event date, its recorded date, the change, and the text. Removed text is struck through, with an explanation.
- **Age panel:** Age (precise chronological), Corrected age (if on), and Age used for milestones (basis labelled).
- **Home:** "Record a milestone" is now a solid, active quick action; the others stay dashed placeholders.

## Growth UX refinement components (Checkpoint 2a)
- **Growth section card:** title, the approved subtitle (body text color, so the purpose reads first), and one muted live line, such as “6 of 72 recorded as achieved”, “72 milestones apply now” or “Now: 15–18 months”. Goals & Exercises stays a dashed placeholder with the “Coming soon” tag.
- **“Coming soon” tag:** replaces “Not available yet · Checkpoint N” everywhere. No development terms appear in the app.
- **Guide (What to Expect):** a reading layout, not a list of controls.
  - Header (outside the compared content): child, age line, range select, Earlier/Next, preview banner.
  - Content:
    - an “About this guide” card;
    - the range title and the “This range includes CDC's … milestones” line;
    - the overview (16px, line-height 1.6, max about 38em) with its “Written for this app” label;
    - per CDC age, the five areas with a small heading, a one-line muted explanation (first CDC age only) and a plain bulleted list of CDC milestones, with no badges or tap targets;
    - the gross/fine note, “Looking ahead”, the collapsed “How the age ranges work”, and the Sources and parent-note card.
- **Neighbour card (empty ranges and Looking ahead):** compact; the CDC age (“Most recent CDC age: by 18 months”), “Shown in [range]”, and an outline “Open” button. It is educational navigation only.
- **Library view control:** a segmented control, “Up to now (N)”, “Upcoming (N)”, “All 159”, with a muted catalog label below it, such as “All 159 CDC milestones · 157 shown · 2 hidden”. A one-line info note explains recording. Rows are grouped under CDC-age headings in the view's order.
- **Progress:**
  - a headline card (“Milestones recorded as achieved” and the overall line);
  - a “What updates Progress?” card (icon list and a secondary “Record in the Milestone Library” button);
  - a “By area” card whose rows are links with a hover/focus tint;
  - the safeguard;
  - collapsed “By CDC age” and “How is this calculated?” accordions;
  - the age panel;
  - a one-line “coming soon” note.
- **Confirmation banner:** the check icon, the effect message and, when progress was affected, a “See Progress” link.
