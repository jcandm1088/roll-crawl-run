# Design System (Checkpoint 1)

The source of truth for colors is `src/ui/theme/themes.ts`. Spacing, type, and component styles are in `src/ui/styles/base.css`.

## Direction
Premium, warm, calm, and adult. Neutral backgrounds, one confident primary color per theme, soft rounded cards, generous spacing, serif display type paired with a clean sans. There are no cartoons or illustrations beyond the app mark. Progress and status will never use red or amber "warning" colors.

## Brand
- **Wordmark:** "Roll, Crawl, **RUN**" in Fraunces 500, with "RUN" in semibold in the theme's primary color. The subtitle "TRACKING GROWTH TOGETHER" sits below in Inter 600, uppercase, tracked at 0.16em. Capitalization is preserved exactly.
- **App mark:** three circles of increasing size rising along a soft curved path (a quiet nod to rolling, crawling, running). It sits on a rounded square and uses theme colors in the app. The installed icon uses fixed Earth colors.
- Icon files: `public/icons/` (192, 512, maskable 512 with a 12% safe-zone inset, Apple touch 180, SVG favicon). They are generated by `npm run icons`.

## Typography
| Role | Font | Size / line height |

| Section heading | Fraunces 500 | 22px / 1.25 |
| Card title | Inter 600 | 18px / 1.35 |
| Body | Inter 400 | 16px / 1.5 (16px also prevents iOS zoom on inputs) |
| Secondary | Inter 400 | 14px / 1.45 |
| Caption, badge, eyebrow | Inter 600 | 13px (eyebrow uppercase, 0.08em tracking) |

Fonts are self-hosted (latin subset, woff2) and work offline.

## Spacing, shape, elevation
- 4px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 56. The phone side gutter is 16px and card padding is 20px.
- Radii: 10 (small), 14 (inputs, choice cards), 20 (cards), 24 (sheet top corners), and pill for buttons, chips, and segmented controls.
- Shadows are soft and tinted by the theme: `card` (1px plus a 20px blur) and `float` (sheets, banners).
- Content is centered with a 560px maximum width on tablets and desktops.

## Components
- **Buttons:** pill-shaped, 48px tall. Variants: primary (filled), secondary (soft tint), outline, ghost (text), and danger. Danger uses a neutral outline rather than red, because destructive actions are protected by typed confirmation instead of alarming color. Pressing a button scales it slightly (0.98).
- **Inputs:** 48px tall, 1.5px strong outline, 14px radius, and a 3px focus halo. Errors show an icon and text, never color alone.
- **Choice cards** (radio-like) and **segmented controls** show selection with a soft tint and a check mark.
- **Cards:** surface background, hairline border, soft shadow. **Placeholder cards** use a dashed outline, no shadow, and a "Not available yet · Checkpoint N" tag, so they never look like working features.
- **Top bar:** sticky and translucent, with a backdrop blur. It holds the app mark (or a back button), the active-child chip (avatar, name, chevron), and the Settings button.
- **Bottom navigation:** five tabs with an icon and a text label, each at least 64px tall. The active tab uses the primary color, semibold text, and a small indicator bar, so it doesn't rely on color alone.
- **Sheets:** bottom sheets on phones and centered dialogs on wider screens. They close with Escape or a tap on the dimmed background.
- **Icons:** Lucide line icons at a 1.75px stroke, 22–24px.

## Motion & accessibility
- Screens fade and rise slightly on entry (240ms), sheets slide up, and theme colors cross-fade. All motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Layouts respect safe areas (`viewport-fit=cover` with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` padding).
- Touch targets are at least 48px. The focus ring is 3px in the theme's focus color.
